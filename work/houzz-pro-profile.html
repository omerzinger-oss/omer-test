Houzz 2019 — 2021

Houzz Pro Profile

Redesigning the professional profile experience to maximize visibility, showcase portfolios, and drive more quality connections between homeowners and professionals.

Role Senior Product Designer
Platform Web (Responsive)
Method A/B Testing
Users Millions of homeowners
The Problem

Critical content hidden below the fold

The Houzz Pro Profile is where professionals showcase their work portfolios, achievements, certifications, and reviews — essentially their digital storefront to attract potential clients. But data revealed a significant problem: 57% of Houzz desktop users were on low-resolution screens (1280px), meaning a majority of profile-converting information was being lost below the fold.

Even more concerning, 12.4% of users mostly didn't scroll below the fold. The old profile's 3-rail content layout created a condensed, bulky organization that made it difficult to scan at a glance and responded poorly across screen sizes.

Research

Understanding what professionals value most

To ground our redesign in real data, we interviewed 10 professionals from similar categories — design-build firms, remodelers, and general contractors. We used a card sorting exercise where interviewees ranked the most important information their profile should promote.

The profile needed to better align with homeowners looking for specific projects that pros offer, while promoting the most pertinent content — particularly project photos and reviews, which were the strongest drivers of conversion.

01

Data analysis

Analyzed screen resolution data and scroll depth patterns to quantify how much converting content was being missed by the majority of users.

02

Pro interviews & card sorting

Conducted structured interviews with 10 professionals, using card sorting to understand content priority from the supply side of the marketplace.

03

Layout reorganization

Moved from a 3-rail layout to a cleaner, more scannable structure that surfaces portfolio photos and reviews above the fold on all screen sizes.

04

KPI-driven validation

Measured success through time on site, "Contact Pro" clicks, and quality connections — the metrics that matter for both pros and homeowners.

Impact

More visibility, more connections

The redesigned profile dramatically improved content visibility for the 57% of users on low-res screens. By restructuring the layout and prioritizing the most converting content above the fold, we saw meaningful improvements in engagement and quality connections between homeowners and professionals.
