Melio × Shopify 2022

Shopify Bill Pay

Designing a seamless bill payment experience within Shopify's ecosystem, integrating Melio's powerful payment flow with Shopify's Polaris design system for millions of merchants.

Role Lead Product Designer
Team Melio Partnerships
Platform Web (Shopify Admin)
Duration 6 Months
The Challenge

Bringing B2B payments to where merchants already work

Small business owners using Shopify needed a way to manage bill payments without leaving the platform they already know and trust. The challenge was to create a seamless Shopify-native experience that felt like it was built by Shopify itself, while leveraging Melio's powerful payment infrastructure behind the scenes.

This required close collaboration between two companies with different design systems, product cultures, and technical constraints — all while meeting strict Shopify requirements for quality and consistency.

Approach

Designing within Shopify's Polaris ecosystem

Meeting Shopify's strict design requirements meant fully adopting their Polaris design system. We adopted the familiar table view layout from Shopify's "Products" and "Orders" tabs and applied the same methodology to the Melio payment flow.

A key design decision was reimagining Melio's multi-page wizard flow into a single-page step-by-step experience. Unlike Melio's standalone product where users navigate through dedicated pages, in Shopify, users can complete the entire payment flow within the same page — matching the mental model Shopify merchants already have.

01

Cross-company alignment

Defined the work method and execution process with stakeholders from both Melio and Shopify, establishing shared design principles and review cadences.

02

Polaris pattern audit

Deep-dived into Shopify's Polaris design system to identify common patterns, ensuring every interaction felt native to the Shopify ecosystem.

03

Flow redesign

Reimagined Melio's multi-step wizard into a single-page flow that aligns with Shopify's interaction paradigms while maintaining payment clarity.

04

Iterative testing & launch

Conducted merchant testing, iterated on feedback, and launched to Shopify's massive merchant base with tracked KPIs for success measurement.

Results

Measurable impact from day one

The launch was tracked against key KPIs including onboarded users, completed payments, bills created, and monthly active users. The seamless integration led to strong adoption, with merchants appreciating not having to leave their familiar Shopify admin environment to manage payments.
