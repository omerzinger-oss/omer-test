First Financial Bank 2022

First Financial Bank

Reimagining the digital banking experience with a modern, accessible interface that builds trust and simplifies financial management for everyday users.

Role Product Designer
Platform iOS & Android
Focus Digital Banking
Scope End-to-End Redesign
The Challenge

Modernizing a traditional banking experience

First Financial Bank needed to bridge the gap between their established reputation for trust and reliability and the modern digital expectations of today's banking customers. The existing digital experience felt dated and didn't reflect the quality of service their customers experienced in branch.

The goal was to create a mobile banking experience that felt contemporary and intuitive while maintaining the sense of security and trustworthiness that banking customers expect.

Approach

Trust through clarity

Banking apps deal with people's most sensitive information — their money. Every design decision was made with trust and clarity as the primary lens. We stripped away unnecessary complexity, used clear visual hierarchies, and ensured that users always knew exactly where they stood financially.

Accessibility was a cornerstone of the redesign. With a diverse user base spanning multiple age groups, the interface needed to be intuitive for digital natives and approachable for those less comfortable with technology.

01

User research & personas

Conducted interviews across demographics to understand the diverse needs, pain points, and mental models of banking customers.

02

Information architecture

Restructured the app's navigation and content hierarchy to prioritize the most-used actions and reduce cognitive load.

03

Visual design system

Crafted a modern design system that balances professionalism with approachability, using color, typography, and spacing deliberately.

04

Accessibility audit & testing

Ensured WCAG compliance throughout, with focus on contrast ratios, touch targets, and screen reader compatibility.

Impact

A modern banking experience customers trust

The redesigned app delivered a significantly improved user experience, with measurable improvements in task completion and customer satisfaction scores. The modern interface helped position First Financial Bank as a forward-thinking institution while maintaining their core values.
