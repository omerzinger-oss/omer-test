Melio Payments

Leading design for Melio's partnerships group — managing 3 product design teams to build smart, simple B2B payment solutions that keep small businesses in business.

Role Design Group Manager
Team 3 Design Teams
Focus B2B Partnerships
Duration 2+ Years
The Vision

Democratizing B2B payments for small businesses

Melio is on a mission to keep small businesses in business by providing a smart, simple B2B payments solution. As Design Group Manager for the partnerships division, I was responsible for defining the product design strategy across multiple partner integrations, ensuring a cohesive experience regardless of where merchants encounter Melio.

The $25 trillion B2B payments market was ripe for disruption. Small and medium-sized businesses needed a way to transfer and receive payments quickly and easily — providing better control over cash flow, eliminating late payment costs, and saving valuable time.

Leadership

Building and empowering design teams

Managing three product design teams required not just design expertise, but strategic vision and people management. I focused on creating clear design principles, establishing efficient workflows, and fostering a culture of user-centered thinking across all partnership projects.

Each team tackled different partnership integrations — from major platforms like Shopify to financial institutions — while maintaining a unified Melio design language that adapted elegantly to each partner's ecosystem.

Impact

Scaling design across partnerships

Under my leadership, the partnerships design group successfully launched multiple partner integrations, each contributing to Melio's growth in the SMB payments space. By creating scalable design patterns and frameworks, we reduced time-to-launch for new partnerships while maintaining quality.
