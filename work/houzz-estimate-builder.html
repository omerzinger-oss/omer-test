Houzz 2020

Houzz Estimate Builder

Creating an intuitive estimate builder tool for home remodeling professionals, streamlining the quoting process and improving client communication.

Role Senior Product Designer
Product Houzz Pro
Platform Web & Mobile
Category SaaS / Pro Tools
The Challenge

Professionalizing the estimate process

Home remodeling professionals were spending hours creating estimates using spreadsheets, word processors, or even pen and paper. The results were often inconsistent, unprofessional-looking, and difficult to manage as projects evolved. Pros needed a purpose-built tool that was both powerful enough for complex projects and simple enough to use on the go.

The estimate builder had to handle the complexity of remodeling projects — multiple line items, material categories, labor costs, markups, and taxes — while presenting a clean, professional document to homeowner clients.

Approach

Complexity made simple

The key design challenge was managing progressive disclosure — showing just enough at each step so pros wouldn't feel overwhelmed, while providing access to the full power of the tool when needed. Line items, categories, markups, and taxes all had to be easy to add and adjust.

We focused on making the most common workflows lightning-fast: adding line items, adjusting quantities, and sending polished estimates to clients — all achievable in just a few taps on mobile.

01

Workflow mapping

Mapped the end-to-end estimating workflow from initial client consultation to signed approval, identifying pain points at each stage.

02

Progressive disclosure design

Designed a layered interface where basic estimates are quick to create, while advanced features (markups, templates, categories) are accessible without cluttering the default experience.

03

Client-facing output

Designed polished, branded estimate documents that professionals could be proud to share with homeowner clients.

04

Cross-platform optimization

Ensured the full estimate creation and management flow worked seamlessly on both web and the Houzz Pro mobile app.

Impact

Empowering pros to close more deals

The Estimate Builder became one of the most-used features in the Houzz Pro suite, helping professionals present more polished proposals and close deals faster. By reducing the friction of estimate creation, we freed up professionals to spend more time doing what they do best — designing and building beautiful spaces.
